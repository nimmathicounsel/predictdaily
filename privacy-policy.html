Privacy Policy

PredictDaily respects your privacy. This application is designed to provide daily star-based predictions while collecting minimal user data.

By using PredictDaily, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collected

PredictDaily may collect and store the following information locally on your device:

  • Birth star (Nakshatra) information
  • Names entered for identifying family members or friends
  • Preferred language settings
  • Location information used for star calculations
  • Application display preferences and settings

How Information Is Used

The information provided by the user is used only for:

  • Calculating daily star-based predictions
  • Displaying predictions for multiple people
  • Improving the usability of the application
  • Providing location-based astronomical calculations

Data Storage

PredictDaily is designed with privacy in mind. User information is primarily stored locally on the device.

Personal information entered into the app is not sold, shared, rented, or distributed to third parties.

Location Information

If location access is granted, the application uses the location only for determining accurate daily star calculations based on the user's region and time zone.

Location information is not used for tracking purposes.

Third-Party Services

PredictDaily may use platform services provided by Android, Google Play Services, or Flutter-related libraries for normal app functionality.

These services may collect technical information as governed by their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

PredictDaily does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
