Welcome to PredictDaily

PredictDaily is designed to provide traditional astrology-based guidance in a simple and accessible manner.

Help

The first step is to enter one or more Janma Nakshatrams in the Settings page. Enter the person's name, choose the corresponding birth star, and click "Add Person". You may repeat this process to add multiple family members or friends.

Added people can be removed at any time, and their display order may also be rearranged for convenience.

Additional options available in the Settings page include:

  • Selecting your preferred language
  • Choosing your location
  • Configuring how many days of predictions should be displayed
  • Changing the number of days shown per row

On the main page, the list of added people is displayed. Clicking a person's name opens the prediction calendar. Color indicators are used to quickly show whether a day may be favorable, average, or challenging.

Selecting a colored date block opens the detailed prediction page. Since the Nakshatra may change during the day, the date block is visually divided according to the Nakshatra ending time. Clicking the Nakshatra names will display the corresponding predictions for each part of the day.

Traditional Reference

The calculation methodology used in this application is inspired by the principles explained in the book Kudumba Jyothitam (Page 176).

We express our sincere gratitude to the respected authors Potheri Shree Ramaiyankar and Keezhathur Shree Shreenivasacharyar for preserving and sharing this valuable traditional knowledge.

Astronomical Data

Planetary and astronomical calculations are powered by the Swiss Ephemeris software library, widely recognized for its accuracy and reliability in astrological computations.
